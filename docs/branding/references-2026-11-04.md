# Design references — 2026-11-04

The existing OpenTeleport landing and Shift identity remain selected. These references inform future campaign material and interaction polish.

| Resource | What it contains | Useful for OpenTeleport |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/) | 600,000+ shipped app/site screens, flows and an MCP entry point | Onboarding, transfer previews and reports when a visual app is built |
| [Design Spells](https://designspells.com/) | Recorded interaction, motion and small UI details | A memorable transfer/completion animation |
| [Recent](https://recent.design/) | Websites, Open Graph images, icons and launch videos | Consistent posts and short launch clips |
| [Brand Guidelines](https://www.brandguidelines.net/) | Curated brand systems and templates | One logo, palette, type hierarchy and usage rules |
| [Deck Gallery](https://www.deck.gallery/) | Pitch, sales, guideline and other presentation decks | A short problem → demonstration → result story |

## Concrete examples

1. [Linear sales deck](https://www.deck.gallery/linear-pitch-deck/) and [Linear's animated import progress](https://designspells.com/spells/animated-progress-indicator-when-using-linears-import-assistant): a focused technical product presentation and motion directly related to migration. Best fit for our current direction.
2. [Dropbox by COLLINS](https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/dropbox/), also linked by Brand Guidelines: expressive combinations of color, typography and imagery. Useful as a reference for a bold launch campaign; its palette is not a proposed replacement for Shift.
3. [Cobalt brand system](https://www.brandguidelines.net/templates/cobalt): a concrete example of documenting logo usage, typography, palette and imagery. Useful for keeping all OpenTeleport materials consistent.
4. [Recent OG images](https://recent.design/og-images) includes Agentation, Paper, Notion and Granola; [launch videos](https://recent.design/launch-videos) provides a separate motion library. Useful for varying campaign composition while keeping one identity.

Recommendation: retain Shift and the current landing; use Linear-level clarity, one recognizable portal transition and the existing white/charcoal campaign art. Increase impact through a strong real product demonstration and a short headline. Treat this as a design recommendation, not a redesign performed on the website.
